Before we talk

A few useful answers.

Have something more specific in mind? Talk to our team.

What can the LogicMesh team help us build?

Our core focus is end-to-end embedded product development: PCB design, firmware, IoT connectivity, and prototype bring-up. We also build web platforms, APIs, and mobile apps for connected products. Explore our development services to find the closest fit.

Where is your development team based?

LogicMesh Solutions is based in Karachi, Pakistan. Share your location, time zone, and preferred meeting times when you get in touch so we can discuss how to collaborate.

Can we discuss improvements to an existing product?

Yes. Tell us about your current technology, the issues you're facing, and the changes you want. That context helps us assess the relevant expertise and next steps.

What should we include in a project inquiry?

A short description of your business goal, the main features or challenges, your target timeline, and an approximate budget is a useful start. Include a link to your current product if you have one.
