PCB Design · ESP32 · IoT

PCB Design for ESP32-Based Smart Device

Project details

Production-ready ESP32 PCB with USB-C charging, TP4056 battery management, GC9D01 TFT display, SD card interface, and tactile buttons

KiCadESP32Multilayer PCBUSB-CJLCPCB
Delivery notes & client feedback

Role: PCB Designer and Embedded Systems Engineer.

Designed and delivered a high-performance ESP32 PCB optimized for IoT devices, wearables, and embedded electronics. The board integrates USB-C charging, TP4056 battery management, GC9D01 TFT display support, SD card interface, tactile buttons, and JTAG programming.

Delivered complete schematics, PCB layout, BOM, and pick-and-place files with DRC/ERC verification for JLCPCB assembly. The work also covered component footprint corrections, CP2102 USB-UART integration, and GitHub version control for a compact, production-ready SMD design.

The client rated the collaboration 5.0 out of 5 and said, "Very cooperative and helped develop the PCB during my busy phase. Appreciated his professionalism and recommend him to anyone."

ESP32-S3 Smart Device with LCD & Relay Control

Project details

Custom firmware for Waveshare LCD integration, BLE relay control, and ESP32-S3 PCB layout. Production-ready with OTA updates and deep sleep.

ESP32-S3BLEFreeRTOSKiCadOTA
Delivery notes & client feedback

Client required a complete embedded system integrating a Waveshare LCD display, BLE relay control, and multiple sensor inputs — all running on a custom ESP32-S3 PCB designed in KiCad.

We delivered full firmware (FreeRTOS task architecture, BLE GATT server, display driver, OTA) plus the PCB layout with DFM-reviewed Gerber files ready for factory production.

The client rated the collaboration 5.0 ★ — "the collaboration was a success. The initial phase progressed rapidly, and he contributed valuable new ideas."

Our core focus is end-to-end embedded product development: PCB design, firmware, IoT connectivity, and prototype bring-up. We also build web platforms, APIs, and mobile apps for connected products. Explore our development services to find the closest fit.

Where is your development team based?

Logic Mesh Solutions (SMC-Private) Limited is based in Karachi, Pakistan. Share your location, time zone, and preferred meeting times when you get in touch so we can discuss how to collaborate.

Can we discuss improvements to an existing product?

Yes. Tell us about your current technology, the issues you're facing, and the changes you want. That context helps us assess the relevant expertise and next steps.

What should we include in a project inquiry?

A short description of your business goal, the main features or challenges, your target timeline, and an approximate budget is a useful start. Include a link to your current product if you have one.
